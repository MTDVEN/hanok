art prep
Drop the Recraft exports here — the base plate and the six buildings, all at once is fine.
PNG, JPG or WebP. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; this runs entirely in your browser.

What this does. For a building: floods the background away from the outside edge, softens the cut, then crops tight so the building stands on the bottom edge of its own file. For the base plate: leaves it alone apart from a downscale if it is enormous.

Then: hit save all and move the files from your Downloads folder into art/village/, replacing the placeholders.

If a building comes out with holes in it, its walls are too close to the background colour — drag key strength down. If a halo of background survives around it, drag key strength up. The preview updates live.

If your export already has a transparent background, that is detected and the keying is skipped automatically.
